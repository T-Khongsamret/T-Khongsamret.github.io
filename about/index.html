Biography

Thantham Khongsamret (แทนธรรมก์ คงสำเร็จ) is an undergraduate Electrical and Electronics Engineering student researcher at Kasetsart University, Sriracha Campus, Thailand. His research interests include power electronics, bidirectional DC–DC conversion, converter control and electric vehicle charging. His PSIM-based comparative study of SPS-DAB and asymmetric CLLC converters has been accepted for presentation at IEEE ITEC-AP 2026.

Research and conference presentation

First author of paper P00167, “Comparative Evaluation of DAB and CLLC Bidirectional DC-DC Converters for Wide-Voltage-Range V2G Chargers,” scheduled for oral presentation in session TD05-6 on 3 December 2026, 17:00–17:15 KST, BEXCO, Busan, South Korea. Conference technical program ↗
