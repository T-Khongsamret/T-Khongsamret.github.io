Biography & research interests

Thantham Khongsamret (แทนธรรมก์ คงสำเร็จ) is an undergraduate Electrical and Electronics Engineering student researcher at Kasetsart University, Sriracha Campus, Thailand. His interests include power electronics, bidirectional DC–DC converters, DAB, CLLC, EV charging, V2G, PSIM simulations, loss modeling, measurement and eventual hardware prototypes. He co-authored a DAB–CLLC comparison accepted for presentation at IEEE ITEC-AP 2026. The results are simulation-based and not hardware-validated.
