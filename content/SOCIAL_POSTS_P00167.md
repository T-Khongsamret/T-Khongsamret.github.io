## LinkedIn English — ready for review

I’m pleased to share that our paper, **“Comparative Evaluation of DAB and CLLC Bidirectional DC-DC Converters for Wide-Voltage-Range V2G Chargers,”** has been accepted for presentation at **IEEE ITEC-AP 2026**.

Working with Wason Tanjaroen and Uthane Supatti, this PSIM-based research compares SPS-DAB and asymmetric CLLC implementations across 60 qualified G2V/V2G operating-point pairs. We evaluated modeled efficiency, current stress and the sensitivity of conclusions to numerical and loss-model assumptions.

The results are **simulation-based, conditional and not hardware-validated**.

Research page: https://t-khongsamret.github.io/research/p00167/  
ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0004-9258-6906
