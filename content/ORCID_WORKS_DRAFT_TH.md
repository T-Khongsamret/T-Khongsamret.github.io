Suggested abstract summary:

The work compares fixed-control SPS-DAB and asymmetric CLLC bidirectional DC-DC implementations in PSIM under a 400-V DC link, 250-450-V battery voltage, 1-6-kW power, and both G2V/V2G directions. Sixty operating-point pairs were numerically qualified. CLLC nominally leads 31 pairs, while DAB nominally leads 29 (including 26/30 at 4-6 kW). The conclusions are conditioned on model fidelity, omitted-loss sensitivity, and selected control schedules; no universal or hardware-validated ranking is claimed.
