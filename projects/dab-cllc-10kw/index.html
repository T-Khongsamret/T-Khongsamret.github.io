OVERVIEW

Study overview

This project uses a 380-V DC grid, 280–450-V ESS voltage, and five target powers (1, 2.5, 5, 7.5 and 10 kW). The workbook reports 25 qualified DAB–CLLC pairs. The efficiencies are modeled outcomes, not hardware measurements.

Common basis: 100 kHz · 150-ns dead time · eight C3M0025065D MOSFETs · Tcase 125 °C · transformer ratio 1.0857:1.

CIRCUIT SCHEMATICS

Circuit drawings for the ESS 350-V, 100%-load (10-kW target) case

Dual Active Bridge (DAB)

The DAB contains two full MOSFET bridges (Q1–Q8) linked by a high-frequency transformer and a 9.5-µH transfer/leakage inductance Lk. Lm = 600 µH represents the magnetizing branch, and the transformer ratio is 1.0857:1. A phase shift between bridges controls transferred power.

CLLC Resonant Converter

The CLLC also uses full bridges on both sides but adds two resonant branches, Lr1/Cr1 and Lr2/Cr2, with Lm = 70.395 µH. The resonant tank shapes current and causes power-transfer behavior to depend on inductance, capacitance and switching frequency.

NUMERICAL QUALIFICATION

Verification and refinement process

Five ESS voltages × five load levels × two converter models yield 50 simulation records. Each pair must satisfy power, steady-state, gate-pattern and matched realized-timestep requirements.

25 / 25DAB qualified
25 / 25CLLC qualified
25 / 25Paired results
01 · Common conditions

Fix 380-V DC grid, 100 kHz, 150-ns dead time, device class and loss scope. Magnetizing and resonant components remain implementation-specific.

02 · Power targeting

Use PSIM-evaluated phase-shift search; unsuccessful attempts remain part of the project audit trail.

03 · Settling verification

Some CLLC cases require checked saved-state continuations until periodic steady-state criteria are met.

04 · Paired verification

Match the realized simulation timestep and recompute averages, RMS currents and loss quantities from the source waveforms.

Initial screening yielded DAB 23/25 and CLLC 21/25, with only 20 matched pairs. Phase search, settling and realized-timestep checks were subsequently refined without relaxing the acceptance criteria.

SIMVIEW / MEASUREMENT

Waveform measurements and efficiency definitions

Read each case's steady-state measurement window from the DAB or CLLC workbook tab rather than assuming a common absolute time for all cases.

Port power

Pin = Avg(VDC_GRID × I_GRID_INTO_CONV)
Pout = Avg(VESS_BAT × I_ESS_ABSORB)

Average the instantaneous power product, not RMS power or Avg(Pout/Pin).

Two efficiency measures

Port η = 100 × Pout / Pin
Modeled η = 100 × Pout / (Pout + Pcon + Psw + Pgate)

The two measures may differ because the modeled loss ledger is not fully fed back to the electrical network.

Current and time window

200 µs = 20 cycles at 100 kHz
Primary RMS = RMS(IL − Im)

Subtract waveforms before RMS; never subtract separate RMS readings.

Gate-drive estimate

8 × 108 nC × 19 V × 100 kHz = 1.6416 W

The 19-V swing is a campaign assumption, not a measured gate swing or device maximum rating.

The low-V turn-on fraction is a diagnostic using |Vds| ≤5% of the rail before the gate edge; it does not prove full ZVS without current polarity, Coss charge and dead-time evaluation.

Working with native PSIM and Simview files locally

The workbook contains Windows .lnk shortcuts targeting local PSIM.exe and Simview.exe installations. These shortcuts and raw/source junctions are not portable web links.

With the native project package on the original machine, open case-specific .psimsch in PSIM and .smv in Simview. For LoadFlag=1 models, restore the original INPUT INITIAL_STATE.ssf before rerunning; keep new outputs separate.

The public website currently hosts the workbook, PDFs and circuit images, not a complete portable 50-case .smv/.psimsch package.

INTERPRETATION

Interpretation limits

Modeled efficiency is based on the included loss ledger in the workbook (including conduction, switching and gate estimates). Full magnetic, passive and auxiliary losses are not covered; the nominal ranking may shift under different assumptions.

Relationship to IEEE paper P00167

This is a distinct 10-kW-class, 380-V/100-kHz/dead-time-150-ns PSIM study. Paper P00167 uses a 400-V DC link, 1–6 kW power range and different converter models/control conditions. Their numerical efficiency results must not be pooled as one experiment.

Losses not yet evaluated

Transformer copper/core, external/resonant inductors, capacitor ESR, driver quiescent, auxiliary and cooling losses are not fully evaluated; junction temperature and full ZVS have not been experimentally validated.

Tcase = 125 °C is a modeled boundary condition. Modeled Tj is not a measured junction temperature. Device stress screening is not an SOA/thermal hardware design.
