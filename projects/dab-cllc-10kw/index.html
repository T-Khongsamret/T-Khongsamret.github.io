OVERVIEW

Study overview

This project uses a 380-V DC grid, 280–450-V ESS voltage, and five target powers (1, 2.5, 5, 7.5 and 10 kW). The workbook reports 25 qualified DAB–CLLC pairs. The efficiencies are modeled outcomes, not hardware measurements.

CIRCUIT SCHEMATICS

Circuit drawings for the ESS 350-V, 100%-load (10-kW target) case

Dual Active Bridge (DAB)

The DAB contains two full MOSFET bridges (Q1–Q8) linked by a high-frequency transformer and a 9.5-µH transfer/leakage inductance Lk. Lm = 600 µH represents the magnetizing branch, and the transformer ratio is 1.0857:1. A phase shift between bridges controls transferred power.

CLLC Resonant Converter

The CLLC also uses full bridges on both sides but adds two resonant branches, Lr1/Cr1 and Lr2/Cr2, with Lm = 70.395 µH. The resonant tank shapes current and causes power-transfer behavior to depend on inductance, capacitance and switching frequency.

INTERPRETATION

Interpretation limits

Modeled efficiency is based on the included loss ledger in the workbook (including conduction, switching and gate estimates). Full magnetic, passive and auxiliary losses are not covered; the nominal ranking may shift under different assumptions.

Relationship to IEEE paper P00167

This is a distinct 10-kW-class, 380-V/100-kHz/dead-time-150-ns PSIM study. Paper P00167 uses a 400-V DC link, 1–6 kW power range and different converter models/control conditions. Their numerical efficiency results must not be pooled as one experiment.
